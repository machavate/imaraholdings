About Imara Holdings

Who We Are

Imara Holdings is a diversified holding company headquartered in Mozambique, committed to delivering innovative, reliable and sustainable business solutions across multiple industries. Our name, Imara, originates from Swahili and means “strength, stability and resilience” — values that define our identity and guide every partnership we build.

Our mission is to connect businesses with opportunities by providing strategic procurement, supply chain management, project support and business development services that create long-term value, guided by integrity, professionalism and innovation.
